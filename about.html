About 45 Networks

Strong and resilient technology infrastructure.

The Inspiration Behind 45 Networks

45 Networks is inspired by the RJ45 connector — the universal interface that powers modern wired networking infrastructure.

Just as the RJ45 connects devices and forms the backbone of reliable network communication, our goal is to build strong, secure and scalable IT environments for businesses.

What We Do

We provide networking, structured cabling, CCTV, LAN and wireless, fire alarm and annual maintenance services with a focus on dependable installation and support.
