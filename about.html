OUR STORY

Inspired by the connector that powers modern networks.

The name 45 Networks is inspired by the RJ45 connector — a small but essential interface behind countless wired networks.

That idea represents how we work: every connection matters. We focus on the details that make an infrastructure dependable, from cabling and configuration to security and maintenance.

Our goal is to provide practical technology solutions that businesses can rely on every day.
