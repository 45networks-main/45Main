About 45 Networks

Infrastructure people forget about — in a good way

We're a Hyderabad-based team focused on one thing: making sure the cameras, cables, network and servers behind your business simply work.

Our approach

Built around the RJ45 — the smallest connection that holds everything together

The name comes from the RJ45 connector at the end of every network cable — a small, standard part that entire buildings quietly depend on. That's the standard we hold our own work to: unglamorous, correctly done, and reliable long after we've left site.

Whether it's a single CCTV camera for a shop or a full structured cabling and server setup for an office, we treat each job with the same discipline — survey the space, plan the layout, install it properly, and test everything before handover.

What we value

Strong and resilient — the standard behind every job

Strong

Cabling, mounts and installations built to handle daily use without loosening, fraying or failing early.

Resilient

Systems designed so one faulty camera or switch doesn't take down the rest of the network.

Accountable

Clear scopes, honest timelines, and a team that stays reachable after the installation is done.

How we work with you

A straightforward process, every time

1. Understand the space

We visit the site to understand layout, existing wiring and what the system needs to cover.

2. Plan and quote

A written scope and quote so you know exactly what's being installed and what it costs.

3. Install and test

Cabling, cameras, network gear and servers installed, labelled and tested before handover.

4. Support after handover

Optional AMC coverage so the system keeps running the way it did on day one.
