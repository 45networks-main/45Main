Full-stack infrastructure

Services built around six ports

Every job we take on falls under one of these six services — separately or combined into a single project, from first survey to ongoing AMC.

PORT 01

CCTV Installation & Surveillance

Camera placement surveys, HD/IP CCTV installation, DVR/NVR setup, and remote viewing on your phone or laptop. Covers offices, retail stores, warehouses, schools and residential complexes.

PORT 02

Structured Cabling

Cat5e and Cat6 cable runs, patch panels and racks, planned around your floor layout and tested end-to-end. Every cable is labelled so future changes are simple.

PORT 03

LAN & Wireless Setup

Switch and router configuration, VLAN segmentation, and wireless access point placement for even coverage — no dead zones on any floor.

PORT 04

Server Installation & Maintenance

Server room and rack setup, backup power and cooling considerations, plus scheduled maintenance to catch issues before they cause downtime.

PORT 05

Network Installation & Maintenance

Complete network builds from the ground up — routers, switches, firewalls and monitoring — with routine visits to keep everything running smoothly.

PORT 06

AMC & Technical Support

Annual maintenance contracts covering cameras, cabling, networks and servers — with priority call-outs so problems get resolved quickly, not queued.
